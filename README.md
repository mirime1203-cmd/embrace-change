# Embrace

**From "I don't know" to your next step.**

Big life changes show you what's ending, not what's next.
Embrace helps people turn an unclear moment of change into their own
direction, and take the first real step.

> *Embrace shines a light on what's already inside you (strengths,
> values, wishes) and helps it grow into a path.*

---

## How it works
1. **Start talking.** Share what's on your mind. No questionnaire, no goal needed.
2. **Sort what's urgent.** What you *must* deal with, and what you *want* for your future.
3. **Picture your vision.** Imagine a day when this change has gone well. It may be blurry at first.
4. **Light and Shadow.** Name what inside you might get in the way, and make your own "If …, then …" plan.
5. **Take a small step.** A conversation, some research, a first try, from the very first conversation.
6. **Reflect and adjust.** What did you learn, and how does it sharpen your vision?

Life moves in chapters: a job loss, a new role, your own business.
Embrace keeps each chapter together, and what you learn about yourself
carries over to the next one.

## What it sounds like
> **You:** I lost my job and honestly don't know what I want anymore.
>
> **Embrace:** That's a lot to carry. Let's first look at what's most
> urgent for you right now.

> **Embrace:** Here's what I've understood so far. It sounds like
> independence matters to you. Is that right? Correct anything that
> doesn't fit.

## The problem
People going through a big life change can see what is ending, but not
what they are moving toward. Without a vivid picture of where they want
to be, the change feels like something happening to them.

Plenty of information exists, but connecting it to your own situation is
hard. Thoughts go in circles, options stay abstract and the next step
stays unclear.

**How might we** help people imagine and speak out their future so
clearly that change feels exciting and the next step becomes their own?

## What we're building
- **Embrace**: the AI companion you talk to. It listens, reflects and asks
  the questions that turn free-flowing thoughts into a vision and a first step.
- **Embrace Change**: the free tracker and the way in. It keeps your chapters,
  vision, steps and insights together.

The main menu: **Heute · Kapitel · ● · Kompass · Entdecken**.
See [docs/sitemap.md](docs/sitemap.md) for all pages.

## Who it's for
We start with adults in career change, in two situations that often overlap:
- **After a job loss:** people who need orientation, and often income, quickly
- **By choice:** people who question their path and want work that fits who they are

What both share: no clear goal yet, and no wish for a long questionnaire.
They want to just start talking.

## What makes it different
- **Unlike coaching:** affordable and always available
- **Unlike job portals and general AI chats:** it remembers your journey,
  shows its reasoning and leaves every decision with you
- **Unlike journaling apps:** it goes from patterns to action, guided by a
  vision and by research on how people actually reach their goals
- **Honest:** names guesses as guesses, so you can correct anything
- **Responsible:** not a replacement for a coach or therapist. If someone
  needs professional help, Embrace says so openly

## Purpose, mission, vision
- **Purpose:** Everyone deserves work that fits who they are, and help
  finding it should be accessible, honest and affordable.
- **Mission:** Help people in career change turn their own thoughts into
  a clear direction and a first real step.
- **Vision:** A companion for any important change in life, and later a
  community where people share their experiences and support each other.

## Voice
A calm, curious friend who listens closely, notices what you overlook
and gently asks: *"And what would a small first step be?"*
