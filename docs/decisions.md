# Decisions – Embrace

A log of product decisions, so that every new prompt and feature builds on them. Newest first.

---

## 2026-11-18 · Light and Shadow is the core method

**Decision:** Every chapter and every weekly step follows four steps, based on the research method *Mental Contrasting with Implementation Intentions* (MCII, Gabriele Oettingen and Peter Gollwitzer).

| Step (internal) | In Embrace | What the light does |
|---|---|---|
| Wish | **Your light:** "What do you wish for in this chapter?" | It lights up |
| Outcome | **Picture it:** "How does it feel when it comes true?" | It turns warm and orange |
| Obstacle | **Your shadow:** "What inside you might get in the way?" | A little night returns |
| Plan | **Your if-then:** "When that moment comes, what will you do?" | The light shines through the shadow |

**Why:**
- Research on job seekers found that positive fantasies alone led to fewer applications, fewer offers and lower salaries (Oettingen & Mayer, 2002). A vision needs a reality check.
- A 2021 meta-analysis (Wang, Wang & Gai, 21 studies) found small to medium positive effects of MCII on goal attainment.

**Rules for the AI (needed for the method to work):**
- The order is fixed: wish and outcome first, then the obstacle.
- The obstacle is internal: a feeling, habit or thought.
- The wish is challenging but achievable. Letting go of a wish that isn't feasible is a valid result.
- The person pictures the outcome vividly; it is not a form.
- The plan follows "If …, then …" and addresses the named obstacle.
- The answers come from the person. The light asks, it does not suggest.

**Naming:**
- The app does not use the name "WOOP". Check with woopmylife.org whether and how the name may be used commercially.
- The app does not use "manifest". It sounds esoteric, and the Brand Definition says Embrace is not esoteric or spiritual. The V4 ritual "Manifest, honestly" will be renamed to **Light and Shadow**.
- A short "Why this works" note names the research honestly.

## 2026-11-18 · The vision guides the journey

**Decision:** Every chapter has a vision. It gives the journey a direction and makes progress visible.

- The vision may start blurry and gets sharper with every experiment.
- The vision may change. Earlier versions are kept, so the person sees how far they've come.
- Progress means: the vision is clearer, the steps lead toward it, or an experiment shows that part of it doesn't fit.
- Action starts in the first conversation, not only at the end.

---

## 2026-11-18 · Life in chapters

**Decision:** Embrace is a companion for every chapter of change, not a single journey that ends.

- A chapter is one change: a job loss, a new role, a move, starting a business.
- Each chapter moves through three phases (after William Bridges): **ending** (night) → **neutral zone** (dawn) → **new beginning** (day).
- The person defines at the start when the chapter is done. Closing it is celebrated.
- What Embrace learns about the person (Kompass) carries over to the next chapter.
- People come back when change happens, not because of streaks. Embrace does not try to maximize daily use.
- Career change comes first. Other kinds of chapters come later.

## 2026-11-18 · Main menu

**Decision:** Inspired by Pillowtalk's bottom menu, adapted for change:

**Heute · Kapitel · ● · Kompass · Entdecken**

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Heute** | The current step and if-then plan, today's question, upcoming moments |
| **Kapitel** | The current chapter with its vision, and all past chapters |
| **●** | The light: start talking from anywhere |
| **Kompass** | What Embrace has learned about the person, as assumptions to confirm or correct |
| **Entdecken** | Exercises and rituals: ideal day, Light and Shadow, letter to yourself, chapter review |

**Why not a copy of Pillowtalk:** Pillowtalk is a daily journaling habit. Embrace is a journey toward a vision. Patterns alone are becoming standard in AI journals; what sets Embrace apart is the step from pattern to action.

## 2026-11-18 · Must and want stay visible

**Decision:** Every chapter has two lanes:
- **Must:** income, deadlines, obligations (for example with the unemployment office)
- **Want:** vision, direction, experiments

Practical worries are never hidden. This follows the value "income comes first; no romantic ideas of self-fulfillment".

## 2026-11-18 · Funding by health insurance: to be checked

**Option:** certification as a digital prevention offering under § 20 SGB V (Handlungsfeld Stressbewältigung) by the Zentrale Prüfstelle Prävention (ZPP). A DiGA ("app on prescription") does not fit, because it requires treating an illness.

**What it would require (criteria as of 17.12.2025):**
- Behavior change techniques from four categories; Light and Shadow covers "goals and planning"
- A pre-registered study with three measurements, showing an effect on stress and on at least one coping strategy, such as self-efficacy
- Minimum human support: a qualified person answers within 48 hours
- Provisional certification for one year is possible with a submitted study design

**Open questions:**
- [ ] Ask the ZPP whether an AI companion can be certified (the criteria don't mention AI)
- [ ] Find a partner with the required provider qualification
- [ ] Decide whether a health framing ("stress management in career change") fits the brand
- [ ] Plan the pilot so that it measures stress and self-efficacy
