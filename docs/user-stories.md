# User Stories – Embrace

**From "I don't know" to your next step.**

These user stories describe what people going through a career change want to do with Embrace, and why. They intentionally leave out how the features are built technically. The decisions behind them are in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

**Target group for the first prototype:** adults in career change, especially after a job loss, but also people who question their path by choice.

## Prioritization

| Priority | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Must** | Part of the first prototype. Embrace doesn't work without this story. |
| **Should** | Important for a complete experience, comes right after the core. |
| **Could** | Valuable, but only makes sense once the core works. |

---

## Must

### 1. Just start talking
**As a** person who has just lost my job,
**I want** to simply say or write what's on my mind, without a questionnaire or a clear goal,
**so that** I can start right away, even if I don't know yet what I want.

### 2. Keep what's urgent visible
**As a** person after a job loss,
**I want** to sort with Embrace what I *must* deal with (like income, deadlines or obligations) and what I *want* for my future,
**so that** my practical worries are taken seriously and I don't feel overwhelmed by everything at once.

### 3. Separate what I said from what is guessed
**As a** user,
**I want** every summary to show what I said myself and what Embrace infers from it, and to be able to correct both,
**so that** the picture of me is accurate and no assumption about me goes unnoticed.

### 4. Picture my vision
**As a** person who can see what's ending but not what's next,
**I want** to imagine and describe a day when this change has gone well, even if the picture is blurry at first,
**so that** I have a direction that guides my journey.

### 5. Light and Shadow
**As a** person with a vision for my change,
**I want** to name what inside me might get in the way and make my own "If …, then …" plan for that moment,
**so that** my vision doesn't stay a fantasy and I know what to do when the obstacle shows up.

### 6. Take a small step from the start
**As a** user,
**I want** to choose a small, doable step in my very first conversation, such as talking to someone, some research or a small task,
**so that** I start learning by doing, not only by thinking.

---

## Should

### 7. My chapter keeps everything together
**As a** returning user,
**I want** my current chapter to show my vision, my steps, my insights and my open questions,
**so that** I can pick up where I left off and see how far I've come.

### 8. See my progress toward the vision
**As a** person on a change journey,
**I want** to see how my vision has become clearer and which steps led me toward it,
**so that** I feel I'm moving forward, even when the change takes time.

### 9. Reflect on my steps
**As a** user,
**I want** to note after each step what I learned and whether my if-then plan helped,
**so that** even a path I drop counts as a valuable insight.

### 10. My Kompass
**As a** person questioning my career path,
**I want** to see my strengths, values, needs and energy givers collected in one place, marked as assumed, tested or confirmed,
**so that** I have my own yardstick for my decisions.

### 11. Find my own options
**As a** person in career change,
**I want** good questions that help me find and compare possible paths myself,
**so that** the options I explore feel like mine.

> **Open decision:** may Embrace also offer an idea when I ask for one, clearly labeled as a guess? See [decisions.md](decisions.md).

### 12. Honest pointer to professional help
**As a** person in a difficult situation,
**I want** Embrace to say openly when I need more support than an AI can give, and show me where to find help,
**so that** I'm not left alone when things get serious.

---

## Could

### 13. Start a new chapter
**As a** person whose life keeps changing,
**I want** to close a chapter consciously and start a new one without losing what I've learned,
**so that** every change builds on the last one.

### 14. A reminder at the right moment
**As a** user with an if-then plan,
**I want** Embrace to remind me of my plan in the situation I named, such as on Sunday evenings, if I choose to,
**so that** I remember my plan when I need it most.

### 15. A letter from my future self
**As a** person at the end of a chapter,
**I want** to write a letter to myself in my own words and receive it again months later,
**so that** I can see what came true and how I've grown.

### 16. My data is mine
**As a** user,
**I want** to view, download and completely delete my saved conversations and insights, including everything in my Kompass,
**so that** I stay in control of my own story.
